1. Overview

CozyClock (“we”, “us”, “the app”) is a local productivity clock for gentle interval alarms, notes with reminders, timer templates (coffee, exercise, focus, kitchen, wellness), and Cozy Steps (today’s step count from your phone or a connected watch).

You do not need an account to use CozyClock. Core content you create — alarms, notes, timer paces, appearance settings, and today’s step total — is stored on your device. This policy explains what the app stores locally, which system permissions it uses, and how third-party services (ads, on-device recognition, optional over-the-air updates) may process limited technical data.

CozyClock does not sell your personal information. We do not operate a user profile or cloud sync for alarms, notes, or steps in the current release.

2. Data we store on your device

Depending on how you use the app, CozyClock may store the following locally:

Category Examples Where stored
Interval alarms Name, start/end window, interval minutes, repeat days, enabled state, vibration, sound (system or the bundled cozy tone) On-device preferences
Notes Title, notes, due date/time, whether the due time includes a clock time, priority, reminder style (notification, alarm sound, or silent nudge), repeat (none, once, daily, weekly), completion status On-device SQLite database (cozyclock.db)
Shared text Text you send into CozyClock from another app, or a cozyclock:// link, used to prefill a new note Held in memory until you save or leave the note; saved notes stay in the local database
Timer templates Saved custom durations / “your pace” values On-device preferences
Settings Language, theme color, light/dark/system mode On-device preferences
Cozy Steps Daily goal (10,000 by default), whether you allowed step access, and a same-day phone-pedometer baseline used to compute today’s count On-device preferences. The count itself is read at runtime from the phone pedometer or from Health and is not uploaded by CozyClock
Timezone (read) Device timezone used only to schedule local notifications correctly Read at runtime; not uploaded by CozyClock

After you allow steps, CozyClock reads today’s step count only. If a watch (Apple Health or Health Connect) has a count, that total is shown. Otherwise the phone pedometer is used. The two sources are not added together. The app requests read access and does not write workouts or other health records.

Join, create, and step-visibility controls are marked coming soon and are not a live social network. CozyClock does not upload your step count, name, or a friend list to its own servers. Any sample group names kept on the device are local placeholders, not other people’s accounts.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s storage, removes this local data from that device. We do not receive a copy of your alarms, notes, or step totals through CozyClock’s own servers.

3. Permissions & scopes (by app nature)

CozyClock requests the capabilities needed for alarms, reminders, timers, today’s steps, ads, recognition models, and updates. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos library, SMS, or Bluetooth. Step access is limited to reading today’s step count, and only after you allow it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CozyClock shows banner advertisements labeled as sponsored content to support the app. Ads are provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

  • Ad networks may collect device and advertising identifiers, IP address, coarse device info, and interaction data about ads.
  • On Android, this may include the advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox AdServices signals used for ad delivery and attribution.
  • Ad content and partners are governed by Google’s policies and the privacy practices of participating networks.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example, reset or opt out of the advertising ID on Android, or adjust personalized ads settings where available on your platform).

5. Handwriting recognition (Google ML Kit)

If you use the pen/stylus pad to create a task title, CozyClock uses Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition to convert strokes into text.

  • Stroke processing for recognition is performed on-device for this feature.
  • The app may download a language recognition model over the network when a model for your locale is not already cached.
  • Your handwriting strokes and resulting task text are stored as part of the local task data described above; they are not uploaded to CozyClock’s own backend.

See Google’s documentation for ML Kit for additional details on model download and processing.

6. Optional over-the-air updates (Shorebird)

Some release builds may use Shorebird to check for and download code patches after install (when enabled for that build). That process contacts Shorebird’s update infrastructure and may include basic device/app version information needed to deliver the correct patch.

Update checks are intended for bug fixes and improvements; they are not used to sync your alarms, notes, or steps to our servers.

Scaffolded networking code for account APIs may exist in the project for future use, but the shipping app UI does not require login, registration, or cloud profiles. If account features are added later, this policy will be updated before those features collect personal data.

8. Children’s privacy

CozyClock is a general productivity utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the app, contact us and we will help with appropriate steps within our control (such as guidance to clear local app data or adjust ad settings).

9. Retention & deletion

  • Local app data: retained on your device until you delete items in-app, clear app storage, or uninstall. That includes alarms, notes, timer paces, settings, the step goal, and the record that you allowed step access.
  • Ads / update / model providers: retained according to each provider’s policies.
  • Health and motion data: today’s step total is read from the system when you open Cozy Steps. CozyClock does not keep a cloud history of your steps. Revoking Health, Health Connect, or motion permission stops new reads.
  • Scheduled notifications: removed or rescheduled when you disable or delete the related alarm, note, or timer, or when system permission is revoked.

10. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke notification permission in system settings (alarms and reminders may not fire).
  • Deny exact-alarm, AlarmKit, or full-screen intent capabilities where your OS allows (timing or alert presentation may be less precise).
  • Deny motion, Health, or Health Connect step access (Cozy Steps will not show a count).
  • Limit ad personalization via device advertising settings.
  • Avoid handwriting features if you do not want ink recognition or model downloads.
  • Delete notes and alarms in-app, or uninstall CozyClock, to remove local content.

11. Security

Local data is protected by your device’s storage and OS sandboxing. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Protect your device with a passcode/biometrics and keep your OS updated.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, permissions, or third-party services change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised. Continued use of CozyClock after changes means you accept the updated policy. Material changes that expand data collection will be reflected here before those features ship.

13. Contact
