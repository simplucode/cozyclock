1. Overview

CozyClock (“we”, “us”, “the app”) is a local productivity clock for gentle interval alarms, tasks with reminders, and timer templates (coffee, exercise, focus, kitchen, wellness).

You do not need an account to use CozyClock. Core content you create — alarms, tasks, timer paces, and appearance settings — is stored on your device. This policy explains what the app stores locally, which system permissions it uses, and how third-party services (ads, on-device recognition, optional over-the-air updates) may process limited technical data.

CozyClock does not sell your personal information. We do not operate a user profile or cloud sync for alarms and tasks in the current release.

2. Data we store on your device

Depending on how you use the app, CozyClock may store the following locally:

Category Examples Where stored
Interval alarms Name, start/end window, interval minutes, repeat days, enabled state On-device preferences
Tasks Title, notes, due date/time, priority, reminder style, completion status On-device SQLite database (cozyclock.db)
Timer templates Saved custom durations / “your pace” values On-device preferences
Settings Language, theme color, light/dark/system mode On-device preferences
Timezone (read) Device timezone used only to schedule local notifications correctly Read at runtime; not uploaded by CozyClock

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s storage, removes this local data from that device. We do not receive a copy of your alarm or task content through CozyClock’s own servers.

3. Permissions & scopes (by app nature)

CozyClock requests only the capabilities needed for alarms, reminders, timers, ads, recognition models, and updates. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos library, SMS, health data, or Bluetooth.

Android

Permission / capability Why CozyClock needs it
INTERNET Load banner ads (AdMob), download handwriting recognition models when needed, and fetch optional code updates (Shorebird).
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used by advertising and networking libraries to check connectivity before network requests.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Show local notifications for interval alarms, task reminders, and completed timers.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM Schedule precise wake-up and reminder times so rings fire at the intervals you set.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Allow reminders/alarms to be restored after the device restarts (together with rescheduling when you open the app).
VIBRATE Vibration patterns for alarms and notifications when enabled by the system/notification channel.
USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Present high-priority / urgent timer or task alerts in a full-screen style when the platform allows it.
WAKE_LOCK (library) Helps background scheduling and ad/network components keep short wake windows while work completes.
Advertising ID / AdServices (AD_ID, Topics, Attribution) Used by Google Mobile Ads for ad serving, frequency, and measurement on Android. See Advertising below.

iOS

Capability Why CozyClock needs it
Notifications (alert, badge, sound) Deliver local alarms, task reminders, and timer completion alerts. Requested when you use notification features.
Network access Ads, optional recognition model downloads, and optional over-the-air updates.

CozyClock does not currently declare App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompts or camera/mic/location usage descriptions, because those hardware/tracking scopes are not part of the app’s core feature set.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CozyClock shows banner advertisements labeled as sponsored content to support the app. Ads are provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

  • Ad networks may collect device and advertising identifiers, IP address, coarse device info, and interaction data about ads.
  • On Android, this may include the advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox AdServices signals used for ad delivery and attribution.
  • Ad content and partners are governed by Google’s policies and the privacy practices of participating networks.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example, reset or opt out of the advertising ID on Android, or adjust personalized ads settings where available on your platform).

5. Handwriting recognition (Google ML Kit)

If you use the pen/stylus pad to create a task title, CozyClock uses Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition to convert strokes into text.

  • Stroke processing for recognition is performed on-device for this feature.
  • The app may download a language recognition model over the network when a model for your locale is not already cached.
  • Your handwriting strokes and resulting task text are stored as part of the local task data described above; they are not uploaded to CozyClock’s own backend.

See Google’s documentation for ML Kit for additional details on model download and processing.

6. Optional over-the-air updates (Shorebird)

Some release builds may use Shorebird to check for and download code patches after install (when enabled for that build). That process contacts Shorebird’s update infrastructure and may include basic device/app version information needed to deliver the correct patch.

Update checks are intended for bug fixes and improvements; they are not used to sync your alarms or tasks to our servers.

7. Third-party services summary

Service Role Data involved
Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) Banner ads Advertising/device identifiers, network & ad interaction data (per Google/ad partners)
Google ML Kit Digital Ink Handwriting → text On-device strokes; possible model download over the network
Shorebird (when enabled) Optional code updates App/version technical data for patch delivery

CozyClock does not include a first-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the current app package. Libraries bundled with ads or ML components may include measurement-related components governed by those providers.

Scaffolded networking code for account APIs may exist in the project for future use, but the shipping app UI does not require login, registration, or cloud profiles. If account features are added later, this policy will be updated before those features collect personal data.

8. Children’s privacy

CozyClock is a general productivity utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the app, contact us and we will help with appropriate steps within our control (such as guidance to clear local app data or adjust ad settings).

9. Retention & deletion

  • Local app data: retained on your device until you delete items in-app, clear app storage, or uninstall.
  • Ads / update / model providers: retained according to each provider’s policies.
  • Scheduled notifications: removed or rescheduled when you disable or delete the related alarm/task/timer, or when system permission is revoked.

10. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke notification permission in system settings (alarms/reminders may not fire).
  • Deny exact-alarm / full-screen intent capabilities where your OS allows (timing or alert presentation may be less precise).
  • Limit ad personalization via device advertising settings.
  • Avoid handwriting features if you do not want ink recognition or model downloads.
  • Delete tasks/alarms in-app or uninstall CozyClock to remove local content.

11. Security

Local data is protected by your device’s storage and OS sandboxing. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Protect your device with a passcode/biometrics and keep your OS updated.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, permissions, or third-party services change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised. Continued use of CozyClock after changes means you accept the updated policy. Material changes that expand data collection will be reflected here before those features ship.

13. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or CozyClock’s data practices can be sent through the support channel listed on the CozyClock store listing for your platform.
